Kalimantan Peatland & Ecosystem Restoration

Executive Project Overview

The Kalimantan Peatland Restoration project conserves over 149,000 hectares of critical tropical peat swamp forest in Central Kalimantan, Indonesia.

By re-wetting drained canals, enforcing strict community forest fire prevention protocols, and stopping land clearing, this project prevents millions of metric tons of greenhouse gas emissions annually.
